The problem

Too many messages. Too little time.

“It's hard keeping track of bookings coming through WhatsApp, Instagram and texts. Some days we're overbooked, and other days we've loads of spaces.”Invented customer quote — independent daycare owner, illustrating a potential problem (not real research).
🐾 How it works

Three straightforward views

01 · Bookings

See the week

Check places left and add dogs when there is room and their vaccinations are valid.

02 · Today

Run the day

See dogs booked in, spot vaccination warnings and record Ate, Walked, Played or Napped with one tap.

03 · Pupdate

Share a dog's day

Preview a single dog's activity notes in the sample owner view. Secure individual owner accounts are planned, not part of the alpha.
