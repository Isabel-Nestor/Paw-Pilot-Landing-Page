Behind the build

Level 7 Technology for Business — student project. This page records the concept and should be updated with actual testing evidence before submission.

Chosen niche

Independent Irish dog daycares with roughly 20–50 daily places. Buyer: owner or manager. Daily user: daycare staff.

Problem (invented)

“It's hard keeping track of bookings coming through WhatsApp, Instagram and texts.” — Invented daycare owner quote, not a real interview.

Technology

PawPilot is a proposed web-based booking, vaccination-checking and dog activity update system. Smaller daycares may currently rely on messages, paper records or spreadsheets; these are possible practices, not a verified claim about every daycare.

Existing services

PetExec and Gingr provide dog daycare management tools. Research check date from Task 1: 19 October 2026. Confirm the relevant features against the linked official sites before submission.

Business model (assumption)

€99 setup fee plus €49 per month per business. This is intended to be affordable to small businesses while funding ongoing support; it is not a live offer.

Alpha test

Describe one thing that actually broke or confused you and what you changed. Do not claim a test passed until you have performed it.

Known scope

The demo uses sample data. The owner view is a demonstration, not a secure multi-owner login system. No waiting-list details are collected by the landing page.
